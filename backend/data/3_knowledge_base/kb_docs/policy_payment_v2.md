# Payment and Failed Payment Policy

## Accepted payment methods
The company accepts:
- credit cards;
- debit cards;
- PayPal;
- store wallet balance.

## Failed payments
When a payment fails:
1. The payment is retried automatically once after **30 minutes**.
2. If the payment fails a second time, the order is cancelled.
3. The customer is notified by email.

## Store wallet
Store wallet balance:
- does not expire;
- may be combined with one other payment method per order.

## Example customer requests
- "My payment failed. What happens now?"
- "Why was my order cancelled?"
- "Can I use wallet balance and my card together?"
- "What payment methods do you accept?"
