# Damaged and Wrong Item Claims Policy

## Damaged item reporting
Customers should upload a clear photo of a damaged item within **48 hours of delivery**.

## Orders under $50
For damaged items on orders under $50:
- a return is not required;
- a replacement or refund may be issued directly after the claim is reviewed.

## Orders of $50 or more
For damaged items on orders of $50 or more:
- a prepaid return label is issued;
- the replacement or refund is processed after the item is received at the warehouse.

## Wrong item
Wrong-item claims require evidence, including a photo upload when requested by the support workflow. Refund approval follows the Refund Processing Policy.

## Important distinction
The **$50 threshold** in this policy controls whether a damaged item must be returned. The **$75 threshold** in the Refund Processing Policy controls whether certain damaged/wrong-item refund claims can be auto-approved.
