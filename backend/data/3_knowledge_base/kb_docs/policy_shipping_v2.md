# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Standard domestic delivery
Standard domestic shipping takes **3–7 business days**.

## Delayed domestic orders
If a domestic order is delayed by more than **3 days beyond the estimated delivery window**, the customer automatically qualifies for a **$10 shipping credit**.

The credit is applied to the customer's account without requiring a support request.

## International orders
International shipments may experience customs delays outside the company's control. The company does not provide delivery-time guarantees for international shipments.

## Example customer requests
- "My package is late."
- "My delivery is three days past the estimate."
- "Do I get compensation for a late shipment?"
- "How long does standard shipping take?"
