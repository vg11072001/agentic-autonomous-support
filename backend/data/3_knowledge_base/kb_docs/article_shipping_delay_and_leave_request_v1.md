# Repeated Shipping Delays and Customer Leave Requests

## When this article applies

Use this article when the customer reports repeated or serious shipping delays and expresses an intention to leave, stop using the company, or take business elsewhere.

Examples:

- "I keep facing shipping delays."
- "My orders are continuously late."
- "I am leaving because shipping is always delayed."
- "I have had enough of these delivery issues."

## Workflow

1. Retrieve the affected order or orders.
2. Check the estimated delivery date and current tracking state.
3. Determine whether the delay falls under the shipping policy.
4. If the customer expresses an intention to leave, treat this as both:
   - a shipping problem; and
   - a customer-retention/escalation signal.
5. Apply `POL-ESC-001` when escalation criteria are met.

## Shipping policy

`POL-SHP-002` is authoritative for delivery windows, delay credits, and international-delivery limitations.

Do not promise a refund, replacement, or compensation unless the applicable policy or order state supports it.

## Response goal

Acknowledge the repeated failure, address the concrete order issue first, and then offer the appropriate escalation path.

Do not respond with a generic "please check tracking" answer when the customer is explicitly reporting a repeated pattern.
