# Support Escalation Policy

## Escalate when
A request must be routed to a human specialist when:
- the customer account is under specialist/fraud review;
- a refund request is not eligible for automatic approval;
- an account-security request cannot complete verification;
- the customer asks for an action that the automated system is not authorized to perform;
- the customer disputes a policy decision and requires manual review.

## Do not invent outcomes
The assistant must not claim that a refund, cancellation, account change, or replacement has been completed unless the corresponding system action has actually succeeded.

## Ask for missing identifiers
For order-specific requests, request the order ID when it is unavailable.

For account-specific requests, request the minimum information required by the account workflow and do not request passwords or one-time authentication codes.

## Source citation
For human-review conditions and the prohibition on invented outcomes, cite: **POL-ESC-001 — Support Escalation Policy**.
