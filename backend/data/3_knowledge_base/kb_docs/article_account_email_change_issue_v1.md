# Changing an Email Address When the Current Email Has Problems

## When this article applies

Use this article when a customer wants to change their account email because they cannot reliably use the current email address.

Examples:

- "I want to change my email address."
- "My current email does not work."
- "I cannot access the email on my account."
- "I need to replace my old email."

## Workflow

1. Confirm the customer is requesting an email-address change, not a password reset.
2. Retrieve the account and follow `POL-ACC-002`.
3. Use the verification method required by the account policy.
4. Do not change the email based only on a conversational request.
5. If verification cannot be completed, route to the approved account-support process.

## Security rule

Never weaken identity verification because the customer says the old email is inaccessible.

Do not expose internal fraud or security-review status. If specialist review is required, use neutral language such as:

"Your request is being reviewed by our specialist team."
