# Refund Processing Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines when refunds can be automatically approved and when a human specialist must review the request.

## Never-arrived orders
Orders marked **never arrived** qualify for automatic refund processing only when:
- at least **21 days** have passed from the estimated delivery date, and
- tracking shows **no delivery scan**.

## Damaged or wrong-item claims
- A photo upload is required.
- Claims for orders **under $75** may be auto-approved.
- Claims for orders **$75 or more** must be routed to manual review.

## Fraud-review accounts
If an account is under fraud review:
- refunds must **not** be auto-approved;
- the request must be handled by a human agent;
- the assistant must not disclose internal fraud-review status to the customer.

Use neutral language such as: "Your request is being reviewed by our specialist team."

## Refund timing
For eligible returns, the separate Return Policy governs the return inspection and refund timeline.

## Important distinction
"Refund policy" is not the same as "return policy":
- **Refund Policy** determines refund approval and review rules for specific issue types.
- **Return Policy** determines return eligibility, return shipping deductions, and the post-inspection refund timeline.
