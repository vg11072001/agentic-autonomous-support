# Knowledge Base Retrieval and Citation Guide

## Purpose
Policy documents are the authoritative source for customer-facing policy decisions. Community FAQ documents are useful for language variation and intent matching but should not override policy documents.

## Retrieval priority
Use this priority when multiple documents are retrieved:

1. Specific company policy
2. Related company policy
3. Account/order state from tools
4. Community FAQ examples

## Citation format
When answering a policy question, cite the policy title and section that supports the answer.

Recommended format:
`[POL-CAN-001 — Order Cancellation Policy, Eligibility]`

For multiple rules, cite each relevant policy:
`[POL-ACC-002 — Email update procedure]`
`[POL-CAN-001 — Cancellation eligibility]`

## Conflict handling
If two documents appear to conflict:
- prefer the newer version;
- prefer a specific policy over a generic FAQ;
- do not silently combine incompatible thresholds;
- escalate when the applicable rule cannot be determined.

## Retrieval examples

### Query
"I just placed an order by mistake. Can I cancel it?"

Retrieve:
- POL-CAN-001 — Order Cancellation Policy
- POL-ESC-001 — Support Escalation Policy

### Query
"I need to update my email before cancelling my order."

Retrieve:
- POL-ACC-002 — Account Profile and Email Update Policy
- POL-CAN-001 — Order Cancellation Policy
- POL-ORD-002 — Order Changes and Customer Request Routing Policy

### Query
"My damaged order cost $60. Can I get a refund automatically?"

Retrieve:
- POL-DMG-002 — Damaged and Wrong Item Claims Policy
- POL-REF-002 — Refund Processing Policy

The $50 damaged-item return threshold and $75 auto-approval threshold answer different questions and must not be conflated.
