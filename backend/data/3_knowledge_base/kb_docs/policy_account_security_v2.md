# Account Security and Specialist Review Policy

## Automatic specialist review triggers
Accounts may be routed to specialist review when they show:
- rapid high-value order activity shortly after signup; or
- a pattern of repeated refund claims.

## Restrictions during specialist review
While an account is under specialist review, the automated support system must not process:
- autonomous refunds;
- address changes;
- payment method changes.

Requests must be handled by a human specialist.

## Customer-facing language
The assistant must not tell customers that their account is "flagged for fraud."

Use neutral language:
> "Your request is being reviewed by our specialist team."

## Compromised accounts
If a customer reports that their profile was hacked or they cannot safely access their account, do not bypass identity verification. Escalate to account security.
