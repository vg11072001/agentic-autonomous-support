# Cancellation Because a Better Price Was Found Elsewhere

## When this article applies

Use this article when a customer wants to cancel because they found the product cheaper on another platform.

Example:

"I want to cancel because I got a better price elsewhere."

## Policy rule

The reason for cancellation does not change the cancellation eligibility.

Apply `POL-CAN-001`:

- cancellation is available within 1 hour of placement if warehouse processing has not started;
- once processing has started, cancellation is unavailable through customer support;
- after cancellation becomes unavailable, guide the customer to the applicable return process after delivery.

## Retrieval rule

Do not retrieve competitor, pricing, or general FAQ content as the primary source.

The customer's reason is context. The order's age and processing status determine the action.

## Response behavior

Ask for or retrieve the order ID, then check its current status.

Do not promise cancellation merely because the customer found a lower price elsewhere.
