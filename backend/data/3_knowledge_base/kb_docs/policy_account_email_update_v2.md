# Account Profile and Email Update Policy

## Purpose
Customers may update eligible profile information, including their email address, from Account Settings.

## Email update procedure
1. Sign in to the customer account.
2. Open **Account Settings** or **Profile**.
3. Select **Email Address**.
4. Enter the new email address.
5. Save the change and complete any verification step presented by the account system.

## Security requirement
If the customer cannot access the account, reports that the profile was hacked, or cannot complete the verification process, the request must be escalated to the account security team rather than bypassing verification.

## Relationship to orders
Changing an email address does not cancel, modify, or otherwise change an existing order.

If a customer asks to update their email address **and** cancel an order, treat these as two separate requests:
1. Complete or initiate the email-update workflow.
2. Separately evaluate order cancellation under the Order Cancellation Policy.

## Example customer requests
- "I need to update my email address."
- "I can't cancel my order until I change my email."
- "My old email is no longer active. How do I update it?"
- "Can you change my email before you cancel my order?"
